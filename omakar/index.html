Questions before you begin

What is Omakar?

Omakar is an app for daily devotion. It brings Live Darshan, Aarti reminders, a Sadhana Streak and sacred rituals from community temples to your phone.

Is the darshan actually live, or a recording?

It is live. You watch the aarti as it happens. No replays, no recordings, the real moment.

Which temples can I access?

Omakar works in partnership with community temples, so you can follow your neighbourhood temple and others you love. Confirm list

Can I book a puja from anywhere in the world?

Yes. Sacred rituals are performed at the temple, in your name, wherever you are in the world. Confirm

Where does my offering actually go?

With Direct Donation your offering goes to the temple, and the ritual is performed in your name. Confirm
