# Omkar Website Plan

Concept one for a new omakar.com. The current site does not feel alive. This plan sets out why the new site exists, what it must say, how it should look and feel, and what is still needed from you before it can go live.

## 1. Objective

**Why:** People still have faith, but the temple is far and the morning is short. The daily ritual quietly slips. Omkar brings the temple to the phone so the ritual can come back.

**What the website must do:** Tell the brand story in one scroll, show the app working, and convince a visitor that five minutes a day with Omkar can become a lasting spiritual habit. The single action we want is **download the app**.

**How we measure it:** App store click rate from the site, scroll depth to the habit section, and time spent on the page.

## 2. The story the page tells

The page reads like a pilgrimage, top to bottom.

1. **Arrival.** Ganesha, the remover of obstacles and the god every beginning starts with, emerges from the screen. The site opens behind him.
2. **The promise.** A huge OMKAR wordmark with a gopuram rising through it. One line: the temple, in your hand.
3. **The brand story.** Why Omkar exists. Our grandparents started every day at the temple; most of us start ours on a phone. Omkar joins the two.
4. **The app, ritual by ritual.** Live darshan, guided puja, temple stories, daily sadhana. One phone on screen changes as you scroll.
5. **The habit.** 21 days, 21 diyas. The visitor lights one to feel how a streak works.
6. **The invitation.** A closing wordmark in the Devanagari inspired style and the store buttons.

## 3. Visual direction, taken from your five references

1. **DIVINE, gopuram with wordmark.** We borrow Latin letters hanging from a Devanagari shirorekha bar and a fine line temple drawing above the word. Used in the closing call to action.
2. **PRAYAGRAJ poster.** We borrow ultra condensed giant type with the temple cutting through it, small widely spaced caps, a dotted night sky and a boxed label at the bottom. Used in the hero.
3. **TIRUNELVELI poster.** We borrow cream paper, a high contrast serif in wide caps, the terracotta sun disk, the small caps list on the left and thin rules. Used in the brand story.
4. **Varanasi illustration.** We borrow the vermilion sky, the teal river, red shikharas on the ghats and the flat printmaking look. Used in the habit section.
5. **Red temple illustration.** We borrow terracotta and ochre stone, warm and heavy. Used as the ground of the app features section.

**Palette:** night sky, cream paper, deep ink, vermilion, river teal, saffron sun, kalash gold.

**Type:** Anton for the giant condensed display, Cormorant Garamond for the serif story headlines, Jost for small spaced labels and body text, Tiro Devanagari Sanskrit for the Devanagari accents.

## 4. The Ganesha loading screen

1. A dark night ground with a slow ring of light.
2. A gold line drawing of Ganesha draws itself: halo, ears, face and trunk, crown, then the red tilak.
3. The drawing splits into layers at different depths, so the crown and face come forward faster than the halo.
4. Ganesha keeps coming toward the viewer, passes through the screen, and the hero is revealed behind him.
5. Total time about 3.5 seconds, with a Skip button. Visitors who prefer reduced motion see a short fade instead.

The current Ganesha is a drawn placeholder. For the final site we should use an illustration commissioned in the brand style, ideally delivered as separate layers (halo, ears, face, crown, ornaments) so the depth effect looks rich.

## 5. What I need from you

1. **Access to the current site.** omakar.com is blocked from my working environment, so this concept is built from your brief only. Please share screenshots of every page, or allow the domain in the environment network settings.
2. **App screenshots** of every main screen: home, live darshan, puja flow, temple page, streak or habit screen, onboarding.
3. **Exact feature list** with the names used inside the app, and which ones are live today versus planned.
4. **Brand assets:** logo files, the brand colours and fonts if any, and the brand line if one exists.
5. **Real numbers** we may publish: downloads, rating, temples covered, pujas performed, average streak. Every number on the concept page is marked as a sample until then.
6. **Store links** for the App Store and Google Play.
7. **Temple partners and permissions:** which temples stream live through Omkar, and whether we may show their names and images.
8. **Photography or illustration:** real temple photos you own, or approval to commission illustrations in the poster style.
9. **Languages:** should the site launch in Hindi, English, or both.
10. **Audience:** the primary user (age, region, devotion pattern) so the copy speaks to them.

## 6. Build plan

1. **Concept one** (this folder): one page, all sections, Ganesha loader, built in plain HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript.
2. **Your review:** pick what to keep, swap sample content for real content and screenshots.
3. **Concept two:** refined loader with the final Ganesha illustration, real app screens inside the phone, Hindi copy.
4. **Production:** performance pass (fonts, images), accessibility pass, SEO and share images, analytics on the store buttons.
