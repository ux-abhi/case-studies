Our grandparents began every morning at the temple. Most of us begin ours on a phone.

Omkar was made for that gap. The faith is still there; the temple is a train ride away and the morning is already full. So we brought the temple to the screen you hold first thing every day.

Watch the mangala aarti live from the sanctum. Perform a full puja with every step explained. Learn why a temple stands where it does. Then come back tomorrow, and the day after, until it becomes yours.
