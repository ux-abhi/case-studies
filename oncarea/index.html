A case study about who gets found in timeFellowship research · 24 weeks · 2023

Oral cancer is easyto see, and stillfound too late.

OncareaOral cancer screening for rural outreach camps, built at iHub Divya Sampark, IIT Roorkee. I was the Interaction Designer and Research Lead.

A mouth can be checked in a minute with a light and a trained eye. Yet most oral cancers in India are found at an advanced stage, and fewer than 2 in 100 rural adults have ever been screened. This is the story of why rural patients are left behind, and how Oncarea puts a doctor, a guided camera and a careful AI into the one day a camp is in town.

      03 · Research

      We asked doctors about liability, and patients about fear.

      Semi structured interviews let us follow what people cared about instead of a script. These were the questions that opened the most.

      What the research told us to build: a doctor first system, with limited patient autonomy and human in the loop AI.

      04 · People

      One wants context. The other wants calm.

      Two personas and empathy maps from the interviews. They pull in opposite directions: the doctor wants more information, the patient wants less. The system has to serve both at once.

      05 · What changes

      From an app that diagnoses to a system that supports.

      We leave nothing to chance

      Design focus: a clinically realistic, ethically constrained system for on site screening and outreach programmes.
