Chanakya UG Fellowship · IIT RoorkeeRead the story 24 weeks · App, web, dashboard

Screening fails at the camera, long before the AI does.

Oncarea is an oral cancer screening system for rural outreach, built at iHub Divya Sampark, IIT Roorkee. I was the Interaction Designer and Research Lead. Every decision on this page traces back to our interviews and to published evidence.

See the decisions
Where screening breaks4 links

Oral cancer is one of India's most common cancers, and among the most preventable deaths. Found early, most people survive five years. Found late, only about half do, and in one Indian study seven in ten patients were already late. In villages the specialist is far away, so screening happens at outreach camps: one doctor, a queue, a phone and a few days.

02 · Research

We asked doctors about liability, and patients about fear.

Semi structured interviews let us follow what people cared about instead of a script. These were the questions that opened the most.

What the research told us to build: a doctor first system, with limited patient autonomy and human in the loop AI.

03 · People

One wants context. The other wants calm.

Two personas and empathy maps from the interviews. They pull in opposite directions: the doctor wants more information, the patient wants less. The system has to serve both at once.

04 · What changes

08 · Final screens

Snapshots of every deliverable.

Through several rounds we simplified patient interactions, added scan readiness checks, improved confirmation feedback and reduced cognitive load. For patients, most screens follow the same pattern as the doctor's screening flow.

09 · Style guide

One primary ramp, a ten step neutral scale, four semantic colours and Poppins in six weights. Built with library components so doctor, patient and web stay consistent.
