A case study about restraint in healthcare designFellowship research · 24 weeks · 2023

Screening failsat the camera, longbefore the AI does.

OncareaAn oral cancer screening ecosystem for rural outreach, built under the Chanakya UG Fellowship at IIT Roorkee

Most digital screening tools start with the algorithm. In the field, the problems start earlier: a blurry photo, a missing history, a doctor who won't sign off on a machine's guess, a patient scared by a report. This is how I designed a system where the doctor decides, the patient is guided and the AI only assists.

CHAPTER 01The camp

Found early, oral cancer is treatable. In villages, it is rarely found early.

Oral cancer is one of the most common cancers in India, and chewing tobacco like gutka and khaini is a big part of why. In rural areas the specialist is far away, so screening happens at outreach camps: one doctor, a queue of people, a phone, and a single day.

Oncarea started as a research project at iHub Divya Sampark, IIT Roorkee, with one aim: make early detection and management of oral cancer reachable for rural patients and the doctors who serve them.

The obvious product was an app that looks at a photo and says whether it is cancer. Our research took that idea apart. The hard problems were not in the model. They were everything around it.

CHAPTER 04The people

One wants context. The other wants calm.

Two personas and empathy maps built from the interviews. They pull in different directions: the doctor wants more information, the patient wants less. The system has to serve both at once.

CHAPTER 05What changes

Design focus: turn research insights into a clinically realistic, ethically constrained system for on site screening and outreach programmes.

CHAPTER 06The system

Clinical architecture before any screen.

I mapped the whole clinical journey as five layers held together by three principles. The AI has a place in it, but only in the layers where a doctor is watching.

CHAPTER 07The blueprint

CHAPTER 10Style guide and system

One purple for action, everything else steps back.
