Case study 03 · Carbon credit marketplace

Every credit,accounted for.

A carbon credit is a promise that something real happened in a place like this. The dashboard is where buyers and sellers check that the promise was kept. I redesigned it so they can.

Product designDashboardDesign auditMobile
Orders, invoices and transactions for a carbon marketplace

Screens audited, one by one, before anything was redrawn.

3

States at a glance: pending, completed and settled, the one thing the brief insisted on.

Carbon markets run on trust. When buyers started asking whether credits were real, the voluntary market shrank for two years in a row. Every order that sits in an unclear state, every invoice that is hard to find, adds to that doubt. The dashboard is not paperwork; it is where trust is either kept or lost.

01 · The marketWhy it matters
02 · The brief

Given by the product team for the BNZ marketplace, where buyers and sellers trade carbon credits and I-RECs.

Let buyers and sellers manage orders, invoices and transactions easily.

The line I held on to: clearly distinguish pending, completed and settled. Everything else followed from it.

I went through all 10 screens of the existing dashboard. Tap a marker to see what I found on Order Settlement.
