01Overview

A German course built for people who already live the deadline.

I moved to Siegen for my HCI Master's and learned German the way everyone around me did. I kept a Duolingo streak and still froze at the bakery. Germanly is what I designed after talking to fourteen people in the same position.

Problem

Learners had words in one app, grammar on blogs and phrases in screenshots, and no way to tell if they were ready for the B1 exam their visa or job depended on.

What I did

Research with 14 learners, a scope cut from C2 to B2, a pay once model, nine design decisions, a design system and the full build, in 13 weeks.

Outcome

120+ learners, 3,000+ impressions on day one, and words that stick: 34% better recall than Duolingo in side by side testing.sample

    Where Duolingo is better

    Coming back. Streaks, reminders and a game loop keep more people opening the app every day. Germanly lags here, and I say so openly.

    Where Germanly is better

    What sticks. Articles in colour, reviews timed to forgetting, real sentences and readiness toward a real exam. The next goal is to close the retention gap without adding pressure.
